# Third-party sources

What ggtts takes from other projects, and under which licence. Release archives carry this file, `LICENSE`,
`licenses/` and the licence files beside the vendored sources under `instruments/`.

**Where the texts come from.** `licenses/Apache-2.0.txt` holds the Apache-2.0 terms that kitten-tts-2-cpp,
`kittenml` and S3Tokenizer each ship (each compared with it, ignoring whitespace and section numbering).
`licenses/chatterbox-LICENSE.txt` is Chatterbox's `LICENSE`, the same text as `chatterbox-tts` 0.1.7's PyPI licence
field; `licenses/pyannote-audio-LICENSE.txt` and `licenses/asteroid-filterbanks-LICENSE.txt` are those projects'
`LICENSE` files on their default branches, taken 2026-11-10, as no version of either is pinned by the port.

**Model terms.** Kitten-TTS 2's weights, and any GGUF converted from them, are under the Stellon Labs Community
License, not this repository's; an archive's `README-kitten.md` summarises those terms and links the licence.

**Linked into the binaries.** ggml is linked statically into every engine build; its licence text, with its
copyright line, is `licenses/ggml-LICENSE.txt`, a copy of the submodule's `LICENSE`. Every Rust crate linked into
`ggtts-render` and `ggtts-measure` is listed in `licenses/rust-crates.txt` with its version, licence expression and
licence texts. That file is generated by `scripts/third_party_notices.py` from the resolved dependency tree and each
crate's own source; it is not written by hand, and CI and the pack step fail when a linked crate is missing from it.
