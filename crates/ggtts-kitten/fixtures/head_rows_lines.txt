The little lighthouse kept its lamp burning all through the stormy night. Ships passed safely along the rocky coast.
Every morning the baker opened his shop before sunrise, and the smell of fresh bread drifted down the hill.
By noon the harbour was busy again, and nobody remembered the storm.
The morning bus was late again, so we walked along the river instead.
A small boat drifted past, and someone on board waved at us.
By the bridge, an old man was selling roasted chestnuts from a cart.
We bought a bag and shared them while the sun climbed over the hills.
At the station, the board said every train was running twenty minutes behind.
So we sat on a bench, finished the chestnuts, and watched the pigeons argue.

星期六朝早，我哋一家人坐巴士去咗西貢。天氣好好，海風吹落面好舒服，細佬一路都喺度唱歌。
到咗碼頭，我哋租咗一隻小艇出海。海水清到見到底，有幾條細魚喺船邊游嚟游去，阿媽話好似喺度同我哋打招呼咁。
中午我哋喺海邊一間大排檔食飯，叫咗椒鹽瀨尿蝦、豉椒炒蜆同埋一碟炒通菜。阿爸飲咗兩支凍啤酒，話呢度嘅海鮮比市區好食得多。
食完飯，我哋沿住海旁慢慢行，買咗幾包魚蛋同雞蛋仔。太陽落山嗰陣，成個天都變咗橙紅色，大家都唔捨得走。
返到屋企已經好夜，細佬一沖完涼就瞓著咗。我諗，下次一定要再嚟。
